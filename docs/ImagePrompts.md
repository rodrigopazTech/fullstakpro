# Prompts para Generar Rostros de Estudiantes (Estilo Latino/Mexicano)

Aquí tienes 3 prompts detallados para generar imágenes realistas de tus "alumnos" usando Midjourney, DALL-E 3 o Adobe Firefly.

### 1. Perfil Joven / Estudiante (Para "Carlos Mendoza")
> **Prompt:** Professional headshot of a young Mexican male, approx 22 years old, software developer student, friendly smile, wearing a casual grey hoodie and black t-shirt, modern office background with blurred depth of field, soft natural lighting, high quality, 4k, realistic texture, latin features, dark hair.

### 2. Perfil Profesional / Analista (Para "Ana Sofía López")
> **Prompt:** Professional headshot of a young Mexican professional woman, approx 26 years old, wearing glasses and a smart casual navy blue blouse, soft smile, confident look, tech startup office background, bokeh lighting, realistic skin texture, latin features, dark wavy hair, high definition portrait.

### 3. Perfil Senior / Backend (Para "Miguel Ángel Torres")
> **Prompt:** Professional headshot of a Mexican male software engineer, approx 29 years old, wearing a plaid shirt and glasses, bearded, coding setup background out of focus, warm lighting, approachable and professional, realistic details, 8k resolution, latin ethnicity.
